# BloomScapes demo plant photographs

Real photographs downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. All are licensed for reuse. Display source/photographer/license credits in the demo. Images were resized proportionally to a maximum dimension of 800 pixels and converted to WebP. The 720-pixel crapemyrtle source was not enlarged.

Hosta is identified only to genus in its source; do not assign a specific species or cultivar to this image. Other photos are illustrative species photos, not photographs of particular saleable specimens.
